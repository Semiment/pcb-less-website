  • Allegro A1260: Both are planar-sensing latches in the 3-pin SOT23 family. SC2498T is about 20% more sensitive with a wider voltage and voltage-rating range; A1260 uses the wider SOT23W body, so check the land pattern before switching.
  • Melexis MLX92211 (BOP/BRP ±3 mT grade): Sensitivity matches SC2498T, and it is likewise lateral/planar sensing in a small 3-pin SMD package — the closest match in this table. Melexis also offers a ±14 mT grade, so confirm the grade on your current BOM first.
  • Infineon TLE4961-1M: The TLE496x family senses conventionally through the top of the package (field perpendicular to the package top), which is not mechanically interchangeable with a planar layout, and its sensitivity grade differs — any switch must be reassessed together with the magnet mounting orientation.

What this part offers

  • Symmetrical ±30 Gs latch — alternating south and north poles toggle the output, which holds state when the field is removed
  • Wide 2.8 ~ 40 V supply with 60 V rating, suited to both 12 V and 24 V systems
  • AEC-Q100 Grade 0, −40 ~ 150 °C — covers under-hood and in-door high-temperature locations
  • Open-drain output with built-in current limit and short-circuit protection; the pull-up can drive an optocoupler or MCU pin directly
  • Bandwidth from 20 kHz, leaving ample margin for commutation and speed sensing

Typical Applications

  • Commutation and position feedback for window, sunroof, tailgate and seat motors
  • Wipers, power mirrors, electric park brake (EPB)
  • Low-power BLDC: cooling fans, water pumps, seat ventilation fans
  • Commutation and revolution counting in industrial power tools and home-appliance BLDC
