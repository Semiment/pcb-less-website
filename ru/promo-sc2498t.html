SC2498T

In stockHigh-voltage latching planar Hall switch

Order code SC2498TSO-TR-Q · Marking 2498T · SOT23-3L, tape & reel 3000 / reel

Главная/ В наличии на складе /SC2498T

SC2498T is a high-voltage latching planar Hall switch that senses the field parallel to the branded face — suited to layouts where the IC sits flat on the board and the magnet passes alongside, such as motor commutation and position sensing. Suzhou Boda keeps this type in stock and ships on payment; exact quantities on request.

  • Allegro A1260: Both planar-sensing latches in a 3-pin SOT23 package. The A1260 operates at ±25 Gs (±2.5 mT) versus ±30 Gs (±3.0 mT) for the SC2498T; the A1260 uses the wider SOT23W body, so verify the land pattern first.
  • Melexis MLX92211 (BOP/BRP ±3 mT grade): The ±3 mT grade matches the SC2498T operate point; both use lateral/planar sensing and a small 3-pin SMD package. Melexis also offers a ±14 mT grade, so confirm which grade your original BOM uses before replacing.
  • Infineon TLE4961-1M: The TLE496x family uses conventional top-side sensing (field perpendicular to the package top), which is not layout-interchangeable with a planar-sensing design; its operate point is ±20 Gs (±2.0 mT). Replacing it must be evaluated together with the magnet mounting direction.

Key features

  • Operate point +3.0 mT (+30 Gs), release point −3.0 mT (−30 Gs), typical hysteresis 6.0 mT; alternating south and north poles toggle the output, which holds its state after the field is removed
  • Рабочее напряжение 2.8 ~ 40 V, Max. voltage −28 ~ 60 V
  • AEC-Q100 Grade 0, Рабочая температура −40 ~ 150 °C
  • Open-drain output with built-in current limiting and short-circuit protection; output bandwidth 20 kHz (min.), output delay 15 ~ 25 µs
  • SOT23-3L package, tape & reel 3000 / reel, marking 2498T

Typical Applications

  • Commutation and position feedback for window, sunroof, tailgate and seat motors
  • Wipers, power mirrors, electric park brake (EPB)
  • Low-power BLDC: cooling fans, water pumps, seat ventilation fans
  • Commutation and revolution counting in industrial power tools and home-appliance BLDC
