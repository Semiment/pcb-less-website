Product Center · Magnetic Sensing & Position Detection

Sensors

Latching Hall devices require alternating N and S poles across the sensing face; the output follows the pole transition — delivering stable magnetic switching for BLDC commutation, speed/direction detection and position memory.

Browse by Sub-category

Different sensor types have completely different parameters, so every sub-category has its own page and its columns follow that sub-category actual specification. Click a group name for the group overview, or a specific category for the full parameter table.

Hall switch4 sub-categories · 56 in stock

Latching Hall15 in stockThe output only changes state when the field polarity switches from N to S, so the state latches — a stable switch for BLDC commutation and speed / direction detection. Dual-Channel Hall Effect Output8 in stockOne latching device delivers two channels of magnetic information — speed + speed or speed + direction — for speed and direction sensing, encoder disk detection and actuator travel feedback. Omnipolar / Unipolar / Micropower Hall Switch22 in stockOmnipolar types respond to either the N or S pole, so the mounting polarity is unrestricted; unipolar types respond only to a designated pole; micropower types cut the average current to the microamp level for permanently powered or battery-supplied designs. Used for gear-shift, door-lock, seat and end-of-travel position detection. Two-Wire Current-Output Hall Switch11 in stockOutputs the switching signal as two current levels, so a single wire carries the state and also allows open / short diagnostics; the PCB-less package with integrated capacitor and TVS removes the need for external board components. Used for seat-belt buckle, seat and single-point position detection.

Angle Sensor (Single / Multi-axis)11 in stockNon-contact absolute angle measurement (magnetic and inductive principles) — for steering angle, pedal and motor rotor position.
